## Interpretation

- **KAPLAY movement:** acceptable for normal student-game object counts. The old general claim that KAPLAY is simply slow is too broad.
- **KAPLAY default collision:** the concern is real. It drops below a 16.7 ms frame budget somewhere between 100 and 500 movers in this scene, then degrades sharply.
- **Kontra:** extremely low overhead, including when its Quadtree is used explicitly.
- **Phaser 4:** much faster than KAPLAY in these CI workloads, but its default browser entry is roughly 1.4 MiB gzip, an order of magnitude larger than the small-engine options.
- **LittleJS:** good collision behavior through roughly 500–1,000 movers, with its fixed-60-Hz architecture complicating uncapped numeric comparisons.

Do not choose the school default from this run alone. Round 3 tests KAPLAY's documented broad/narrow phase alternatives before deciding whether collision performance is a library limitation or a default-configuration problem.
